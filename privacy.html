Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Neuro Nibble ("we") runs a private publishing tool, used only by the Neuro Nibble team, that uploads our own videos to our own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

What data we access

When we connect one of our own accounts, the platform gives the tool an access token. It lets the tool upload videos, set thumbnails and captions, and read basic account information such as the account name. The tool does not access data about viewers, followers or any other users.

How the data is used

Only to publish our own videos and to check that publishing worked.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on our own computer. They are never sold, never shared and never sent anywhere except to the platform they belong to (Google/YouTube, Meta or TikTok).

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the platform's settings: Google Account → Security → Third-party access, Meta → Business Integrations, or TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. The tool then deletes its stored token.

Website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.
